# Marc Adamus Videos

Explore Marc Adamus’s landscape photography tutorials in Watchcraft with topic search and chapter navigation. This collection currently organizes 176 videos.

## Open the collection

**[Open Marc Adamus Videos in Watchcraft](https://watchcraft.stream/collections/marc-adamus-videos/)**

The landing page includes an “Open in Watchcraft” button, a download link, and setup instructions. You can share that page with other tutorial owners.

You need your own copies of Marc’s videos. This repository contains collection metadata and analysis, not the video files.

## Choose your video folder

1. Install Watchcraft (version 0.3.1-beta.4 or later for guided setup).
2. Visit the landing page above and choose **Open in Watchcraft**.
3. Select the main **Marc Adamus Videos** folder containing **Part 1**, **Part 2**, and the other Part folders. The folder can be on your computer or an external drive.
4. Review the videos found and start watching. A partial library is supported.

Watchcraft stores collection metadata separately in app storage; no collection manifest needs to be installed beside your videos. For offline playback, download the actual video files to your device or external drive before disconnecting.

If your browser cannot open the app, paste the [collection manifest URL](https://collections.watchcraft.stream/collections/marc-adamus-videos/collection.json) into **Add a collection** in Watchcraft Settings.

## Sharing

The landing page is unlisted: it is not linked from the Watchcraft website’s navigation or featured collection directory, and asks search engines not to index it. It is publicly accessible to anyone with its URL.
